✦ Behind The Shoe
OUR PROCESS
From a sketch on paper to the shoe on your foot — every SOLÉ pair passes through 6 stages of obsessive craftsmanship. Here's how we do it.
✦ Six Stages of Perfection
FROM CONCEPT TO CREATION
01
✏️
DESIGN & SKETCH
Every shoe begins as a hand-drawn sketch. Our designers spend weeks refining silhouettes, proportions, and style lines before a single material is chosen. We draw inspiration from architecture, nature, and street culture.
02
🧵
MATERIAL SOURCING
We source full-grain calfskin leather from certified tanneries, breathable performance meshes from Japanese mills, and recycled rubber compounds for our outsoles. Every material is tested for durability and feel before approval.
03
🔨
LAST CRAFTING
The "last" is the foot-shaped mould that defines the shoe's fit. Our master last-makers carve each last by hand, ensuring perfect anatomical alignment and consistent sizing across every style and size run.
04
✂️
CUTTING & STITCHING
Leather panels are hand-cut using precision steel dies to eliminate waste. Skilled craftspeople then stitch each component together with thread tensioned to exact specification — up to 8 stitches per centimetre.
05
🎨
FINISHING & COLOUR
Edge painting, burnishing, heel-seat fitting, and colour application are done entirely by hand. A single shoe passes through 12 pairs of hands before it receives our quality stamp. No machines allowed in this stage.
06
✅
QUALITY CONTROL
Each finished pair undergoes a 47-point inspection — from stitch density and last shape to colorfastness and sole adhesion. Fewer than 2% of pairs pass on the first check. The rest are reworked until they're perfect.
