ABOUT SOLÉ

WE CRAFT
LEGENDS

SOLÉ was founded in 2012 by two footwear obsessives who believed that great shoes shouldn't be a compromise between style and comfort. Starting from a small workshop in Mumbai, we have grown into one of India's most celebrated premium footwear brands.

Every pair that leaves our workshop carries the weight of a decade of craftsmanship, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to quality. We source the finest leathers, work with master cobblers, and obsess over every last detail — from the last shape to the final stitch.
